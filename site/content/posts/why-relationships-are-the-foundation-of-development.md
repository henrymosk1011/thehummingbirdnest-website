At The Hummingbird Nest, we believe building relationships is the most fundamental thing we can do to support a child’s growth and development.

In order for a child to learn, regulate, communicate, or build social skills, it is important for the child to feel safe and connected. Development occurs within relationships, not in isolation.

Children develop through their interactions with others from the moment they are born, whether it's a baby looking at a parent, a toddler sharing a toy, or a child looking for comfort after a difficult time. In reality, these brief interactions are laying the foundation for future emotional development, communication, and critical thinking.

When a child feels heard and supported, their brain is more open to learning. When there is safety and connection, curiosity thrives. Even the best strategies and academic support might not work without that feeling of connection.

Relationship based strategies like DIR Floortime are so effective because of this. Instead of concentrating solely on behaviors or specific skills, the core of this approach is to join the child in their environment. We follow their example, develop a common goal, and progressively increase participation. Through meaningful, playful engagement, children develop their capacity to regulate their emotions, express themselves, solve problems, and form relationships with others.

Relationships also influence emotional resilience. When specialists and caregivers respond to children consistently and with compassion, children begin to internalize a sense of security. They start to realize that they can work through and overcome their challenges together. This builds children's confidence because they feel supported when they experience difficult moments. Even those teens who struggle emotionally or socially, environments where they feel truly seen and accepted are often the most beneficial for them.

In every service we provide at The HummingBird Nest, connection is our top priority. Our focus is the same whether we are working with kids during 1:1 sessions, facilitated playdates, social skills groups, or when coaching entire families. We create safety, nurture meaningful engagement, and most importantly we build trust.

A child's development evolves more naturally when the child feels connected.

Creating relationships isn't just part of the process, rather, it is the process.
